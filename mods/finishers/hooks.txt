# Finishers. See docs/modding.md for the format.

# sub_08032A88 (blades in contact) resolves a clash with sub_0802FFAC and, when
# somebody won it, shows the damage number with sub_08033630(damage, 80, 60,
# index of the winner). This is the one place that has the damage and the winner
# together, so the mod wraps it: it adds to the loser's blade damage (so a blade
# can break) and knocks the loser back (so it can be rung out).
call 0x08032B3C _08033630 FinishersClash
