## Role

Reads the 32-bit field of the 40-byte record for `a` under the live `unk1818` index.

## Process

- 2026-09-21 — **matched**: the `off = gMainWorkPtr->unk1818 * 4 + a * 40;` local has
  to exist as a *separate* variable and `old_agbcc` is required. That combination
  produces retail's `ldrb r1,[r1]` + `lsls r2,r1,#2` index handling; `agbcc` folds the
  index arithmetic differently and floors at 30/40.
- Earlier attempts with GCC asm labels matched but were rejected (register asm
  ban) — this is the clean semantic equivalent.
