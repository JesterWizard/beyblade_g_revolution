## Role

Timed palette-bank pulse on a text row. Decrements the countdown at `a->unk04`;
on the tick where it hits a multiple of 4 (`& 5` test) it picks a palette bank
(`a->unk00` or `a->unk01`) selected by the high nibble of `p->unk14`, and
recolours the row via `TextRowSetPaletteBank` (through `sub_080705DC`). A
negative countdown instead hands off to `sub_080312D8`.

## Blocker

Register **destination** difference, not a source-shape or layout difference.
Retail keeps the incoming parameter in `r2` and allocates `r1` to the shifted
palette value:

    retail   10b5 021c 107a    push {r4,lr}; adds r2,r0,#0; ldrb r0,[r2,#8]
    compiled 10b5 011c 087a    push {r4,lr}; adds r1,r0,#0; ldrb r0,[r1,#8]

Both are 78 bytes and the control flow is identical; only the register chosen
for the parameter differs, and every subsequent `[rN]` operand differs with it.
This is the same family as the documented `sub_08061308` finding (agbcc
coalesces the load into the register that consumes it), so it is likely not
reachable by source reshaping. The permuter's best variants drifted the other
way (`push {r0,lr}` + `ldrsb`), i.e. it could not reach retail's allocation.
