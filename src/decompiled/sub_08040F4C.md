## 2026-09-21 — allocator analysis

13 differing halfwords, all from one root cause: agbcc gives `done` **r4**, retail gives it
**r5**. Consequence: at `blend1` retail keeps the two zero stores in `r3`/`r4`
(`movs r4,#0` / `movs r3,#0`), agbcc has no callee-saved reg free so it rematerialises the
`strb` value as an extra `movs r0,#0` (+2B) and the `bge` offset shifts.

Everything else (sp-relative `state` access, 0x328 frame prologue, epilogue `movs r3,#0xCA;
lsls r3,r3,#2; add sp,r3`) matches exactly.

Tried, all still 319/336: `f8`/`f16` typed `u8`/`u16` locals in `blend1` (to defeat CSE of
the two zeros), swapped flag init order, `done` declared first, `r0 = r0 + r3` operand order,
combined variants. `old_agbcc` → 315/336. No `-f*` flag helps.

Retail's r4 is used *only* for the `blend1` zero (a 4-instruction live range) — the short-lived
constant must be allocated to a callee-saved register, which agbcc 2.9 does not do once `done`
holds r4. Needs an allocator behaviour we cannot express in C.
