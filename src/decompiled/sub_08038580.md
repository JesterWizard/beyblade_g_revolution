## Role

Marks one of the 16 `gUnk_030003CC->unk20` bits. When the bit for index `idx`
(`(u8)b`) is still clear it uploads a 32-byte palette row through
`_08073C4C(a, gData_05000200 + (idx << 5), 0x20, *gData_080BB8C0)` and sets the
bit; either way it bumps the per-index counter `unk22[idx]`.

## Blocker

Only the **order of two data loads** differs, and it is inside the call setup:

    retail   ldr r0,=0x080BB8C0; lsls r1,r4,#5; ldr r3,=0x05000200;
             adds r1,r1,r3; ldr r3,[r0]; adds r0,r2,#0; movs r2,#0x20
    compiled ldr r0,=0x080BB8C0; ldr r3,[r0]; lsls r1,r4,#5;
             ldr r0,=0x05000200; adds r1,r1,r0; adds r0,r2,#0; movs r2,#0x20

Retail materialises both address constants first and dereferences last; agbcc
sinks the dereference to its use and then has to reload the palette base into a
different register. Same scheduler family as `sub_08061308` / `sub_08062A74`.
