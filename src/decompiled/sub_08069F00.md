## Role

Q8 multiply with floor rounding of `(a * b)`: the product is biased by `+0xFF` when
negative, then `(r << 8) >> 16`.

## Process

- 2026-09-21 — parked at 11/24. `old_agbcc` required (plain `agbcc` compiles 6 bytes
  larger). The missing piece is the `adds r1,r0,#0` copy: agbcc coalesces the product
  and its copy into r0 and emits `cmp r0,#0 / adds r0,#0xFF` plus a trailing nop.
- 2026-09-21 — 13 further shapes: 2- and 3-deep copy chains (`q = p; r = q; s = r;`),
  `r = p + 0;`, `u32` copy, `p & 0x80000000`, `(p >> 31) != 0`, `p <= -1`, split
  `r = p;` statement, braces, `r = r + 0xFF`, `-= -0xFF`, `255` instead of `0xFF`.
  **Every single one is byte-identical at 11/24** — agbcc's copy coalescing always
  wins, so the extra copy is not source-reachable.

## Current state

11/24 same-size. Retail keeps the pre-coalescing copy (`adds r1,r0,#0`) and compares
the original in r0; agbcc merges the two webs and pads with a nop to the same size.
